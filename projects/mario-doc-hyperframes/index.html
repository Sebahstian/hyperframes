16 × 16 = 256 PX Sprite canvas · Donkey Kong, 1981
Hardware Constraint

Sixteen by sixteen

Two hundred and fifty-six pixels, shared with the background tiles. Every feature on the face had to survive being three pixels wide.

01 The Cap Animated hair needed frames the board could not hold. A cap needs none.
02 The Overalls Solid arms vanished into the torso in motion. Contrast made the limbs readable.
03 The Mustache A mouth at this scale rendered as a smudge. A mustache separated nose from chin.
Not a style choice — a memory budget
